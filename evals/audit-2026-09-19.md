## U28: ownership lost under conditional dialog content

The pin uses `@radix-ui/react-dialog` **1.1.15** (`pnpm-lock.yaml:316–318`). `AddFilterModal` owns `activeTabId` and `searchValue` at lines 179–180. Its callers keep the component rendered and toggle its `open` prop: `create-segment-modal.tsx:228–235` and `segment-settings.tsx:223–232`. Inside it, the proposed extracted div at 549 is under `DialogContent` at 543. The local UI wrapper (`modules/ui/components/dialog/index.tsx:70–90`) renders `DialogPortal` and `DialogPrimitive.Content` without `forceMount`. The [published pinned Radix implementation](https://unpkg.com/@radix-ui/react-dialog@1.1.15/dist/index.mjs) gates Portal and Content with Presence on `forceMount || context.open`. Closing the dialog therefore unmounts the proposed new owner while the existing owner remains mounted.

```tsx

  const [query, setQuery] = useState("");
  return (
    <ConditionalContent open={open}>
      <FilterBody query={query} onChange={setQuery} />
    </ConditionalContent>
  );
}
// ConditionalContent returns open ? children : null.
// Moving useState into FilterBody changes its lifetime.
```

Keep DialogOwner mounted; open, type a query/change tab, close until content unmounts, then reopen. Original owner retains query/tab; the recommended moved owner reinitializes them. This is a source-proven defect with a reproduction sketch, not a claimed executed Radix runtime test.
